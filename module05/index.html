What is Plants Versus Zombies?

Plants Versus Zombies (otherwise known as PvZ) is a mobile game created in 2009, where you play as a person surviving the zombie apocalypse by weaponizing plants to beat back the zombies.

About

Plants Versus Zombies is a game with more layers than meets the eye. There are roughly 56 total plants in the game and roughly 28 zombies across all the levels, with all the levels having their own unique modifier

  • Release Date: May 5th, 2009
  • Developer: Popcap
  • Sequels: Garden Warfare, Garden Warfare 2, Battle for Neighborville, PvZ 2, PvZ Heroes

Why do I like it?

For a few reasons:

Simplicity

The easy to pick up and easy to put down gameplay leads to anyone of any age being able to learn and play the game, along with it being a nice and fun game for how simplistic it is.

Nostalgia

The first time I playde this game was when I was really young and the game came out a year after I was born, so I basicaly grew up with the game and all of it's installments, good or bad

Plant Designs

The plant designs in the game make a friendly and welcoming face out of puns and a wide variety of uses. Some are better than others, but they are overall well balanced and each provides it's own unique weaknesses and strengths.

Zombie Design

There are only about half the amount of zombies vs plants, but without the zombies you wouldnt have a game. The art style is really friendly for younger kids, to the point that young me (who was incredibly afraid of zombies) was able to play the game without fear. The wide variety of zombies also goes into making the game a surprisingly complicated strategy game.
